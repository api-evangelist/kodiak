# Kodiak AI

Kodiak AI, Inc. (formerly Kodiak Robotics) is a Mountain View, California company building the Kodiak Driver, an AI-powered ground autonomy system for driverless trucking and defense vehicles. Founded in April 2018 by Don Burnette, Kodiak develops a purpose-built autonomous driving stack that integrates into third-party driving platforms rather than shipping a vehicle of its own, and runs commercial driverless freight lanes with partners including Atlas Energy Solutions, Maersk, Martin Brower, Roehl Transport, JB Hunt and Werner. The same driver is adapted to defense and industrial missions with General Dynamics Land Systems, Textron Systems and the US Army.

Website: https://kodiak.ai/

Backed by: lightspeed-venture-partners (Series B, 2021 — alongside Battery Ventures, CRV, SIP Global Partners and others)
